Point Accrual Rubric Guidelines

1 Point: First missed deadline or minor operational lapse.
2 Points: Missed deadline after supervisor reminder or follow-up.
3 Points: Additional missed tasks while previous tasks remain overdue.
4 – 5 Points: Repeated pattern of negligence, disregard for expectations, or failure to act responsibly.
